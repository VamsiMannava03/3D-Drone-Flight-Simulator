# Demo missions, one command per line, in the console command language.
# Type `help` while the simulator runs to see every command.

# q1: deliver a package to the front door of Elm St 3. It flies in above the
# rooftops, drops down over the street, then flies home and lands.
q1 takeoff 12
q1 goto 20 -10 18
q1 goto 28 -28 18
q1 deliver 28 -25 0
q1 home
q1 land

# q2: survey the city. It clips the hospital no-fly zone on the way, and
# its long route drains the battery, so it reports low battery, climbs
# above the rooftops and returns home on its own before finishing.
q2 takeoff 8
q2 goto 15 25 20
q2 goto 45 25 40
q2 hover 3
q2 goto 75 15 30
q2 goto 75 -25 25
q2 goto 45 -40 20
q2 goto -25 -40 15
q2 goto -10 10 20
q2 goto 40 50 30
q2 goto 80 50 30
q2 goto 80 -50 30
q2 home
q2 land

# h1: the cargo hexacopter lifts a 1.5 kg crate onto the roof of Tower A,
# climbing above it south of the tower first, then flies home and lands.
h1 takeoff 10
h1 goto 30 -8 32
h1 deliver 30 10 28
h1 home
h1 land

# fw1: take off west down the runway, fly a wide circuit around the city,
# then come back and land in the open field to the west.
fw1 takeoff 40
fw1 goto -150 60 45
fw1 goto 100 130 50
fw1 goto 160 -90 50
fw1 goto -100 -110 40
fw1 goto -140 -60 20
fw1 goto -220 -60 12
fw1 land
