# The SRS primary use case, step by step.
# Run it with:  java -jar target/hover.jar --scenario=empty --mission=missions/usecase.txt
#
# 1. Start from an empty world with a ground plane (--scenario=empty).
# 2. Add a quadcopter at the origin with a 0.5 kg payload. It starts at 35% charge, so the
#    wind can push it below 20% during the mission (step 8).
add quad u1 0 0 0.5 35

# 3. Wind: 5 m/s from the west, with light gusts.
wind 5 west 1.5

# 4. The mission: take off to 15 m, fly to two waypoints, drop the payload at the second,
#    hover for 10 s, return, and land. Every step is checked against the drone first.
u1 takeoff 15
u1 goto 40 0 15
u1 goto 40 40 15
u1 drop
u1 hover 10
u1 home
u1 land

# 5-9. The flight runs on the physics engine. Watch the status panel: the mass falls from
#    2.0 kg to 1.5 kg after the drop and the throttle with it (step 7). Fighting the wind
#    drains the battery; below 20% the drone reports LOW_BATTERY, abandons the mission
#    (RETURN_HOME) and lands safely (step 8). Press R (or type reset) to rerun it
#    identically (step 9).
